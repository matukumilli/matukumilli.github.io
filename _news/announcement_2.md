Our work establishing phonon-driven thermophoresis in coaxial CNTs has been accepted in [Nano Lett.](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/full/10.1021/acs.nanolett.5b04014)! It demonstrates crystal momentum → physical momentum transfer, enabling heat-driven directed motion under nonequilibrium conditions relevant to nanoscale motors and quantum heat engines
