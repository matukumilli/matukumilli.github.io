Our work on thermoelectrics has been accepted for publication in [Science Advances](https://www.science.org/doi/abs/10.1126/sciadv.aeh9096)! 🚀 
<br>
In this work, we uncover an intriguing interplay between constrained Cu-ion diffusion, simultaneous hardening of Tl modes and softening of Cu modes, and electronic spectral-weight transfer from Γ to M—revealing how coupled ionic and electronic dynamics can shape thermoelectric performance.
